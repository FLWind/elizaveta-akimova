Hello! My name is Elizabeth Akimova.

![Elizabeth Akimova](/images/me.jpg)

I have loved drawing since early childhood.

Art helps me notice the beauty of the world around me, express emotions, and share my impressions with others.

I am currently studying at [Children's Art School No. 7 in Moscow](https://7.paint.mos.ru/), where I learn academic drawing, painting, composition, and art history. My artistic development has been greatly influenced by my teachers:

* *Andrey N. Kovalevsky*
* *Tatyana G. Kolesnikova*
* *Dmitry A. Burov*
* *Ilya V. Nikolaev*

This portfolio features my school assignments and independent work, including watercolor and oil paintings, drawings, and decorative compositions.
